• From a workstation terminal, run:
 
  ssh -t atom6 /home/atom/metropolis-monitor/scripts/dashboard.sh
 
  Use the arrow keys to move through machines, Page Up/Page Down to scroll, r to
  refresh, and q to exit.
 
  To check the service instead of opening the dashboard:
 
  ssh atom6 '/home/atom/metropolis-monitor/scripts/user-service.sh status'
 
  Atom2, Atom3, and Atom5 will show SSH DOWN until they’re reachable on the
  network.
